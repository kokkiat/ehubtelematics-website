Policies & Support

Our service and device policies are designed to provide clarity and flexibility across both outright purchase and contract-based rental plans.

Refund Policy

Outright Purchase

  • No cash refunds are provided.
  • Defective devices will be replaced in accordance with warranty terms.

Rental Plans

  • No refunds once the service has commenced.
  • Defective devices will be replaced at no additional cost.
  • Early termination of contract may incur penalty charges.

SIM Renewal & Subscription

Outright Purchase

  • Devices include a pre-installed SIM card.
  • SIM subscription is renewed annually via authorised dealers or Customer Care.

Rental Plans

  • SIM subscription is included as part of the service plan.

Delivery & Installation

Outright Purchase

  • Installation can be arranged through authorised dealers or scheduled doorstep service.

Rental Plans

  • Installation is included as part of the plan.
  • Device relocation or replacement may incur additional charges depending on service terms.
